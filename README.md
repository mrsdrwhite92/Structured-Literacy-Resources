Structured Literacy Resources
This repository contains curated materials and lesson plans designed to support students through Structured Literacy—an explicit, systematic, and diagnostic approach to reading instruction.

🛠️ Instructional Sequence
Following the research of Professor John Zachos, these resources are organized by the following foundational pillars:

Letter Print Awareness: Recognizing the physical form and orientation of letters.

Phonological Awareness: Identifying and manipulating units of oral language.

Phonics: Decoding the relationship between letters and sounds.

Syllable Types: Understanding word structure for multisyllabic decoding.
