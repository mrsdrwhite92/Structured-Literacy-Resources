Structured Literacy Resources

This repository contains curated materials and lesson plans designed to support students through Structured Literacy—an explicit, systematic, and diagnostic approach to reading instruction.

Mission

My focus on Structured Literacy stems from a commitment to educational transparency and the belief that every child deserves access to evidence-based reading instruction. By utilizing explicit, systematic, and diagnostic methods, I aim to bridge the gap between classroom instruction and foundational mastery, ensuring that "brain wiring" is supported by proven pedagogical standards.

Curriculum & Integration Map

My instructional framework is built on the Zachos Method, integrated with industry-leading resources to provide a comprehensive literacy experience:

1. The Foundational Sequence (The Zachos Method)

Letter Print Awareness: The critical first step in recognizing form and orientation.

Phonological Awareness: Oral language manipulation and sound identification.

Phonics & Decoding: Explicitly connecting graphemes to phonemes.

2. Evidence-Based Resource Integration

UFLI Foundations: I utilize UFLI for high-frequency word mastery and systematic phonics lessons.

Cox Campus (Rollins Center): Leveraging their "Read Right from the Start" strategies for deep language nutrition and vocabulary.

Logic of English: Implementing the 75 phonograms and 31 spelling rules to take the "mystery" out of English orthography.
